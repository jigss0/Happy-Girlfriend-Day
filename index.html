The Last Page

Girlfriend Day, this year — and every year after it

My Anjani,

I have rewritten this letter more times than I have rewritten anything in my life, because everything I draft sounds too small for what I actually feel, and I have finally accepted that it always will — love doesn't fit neatly into paragraphs, it just spills out of them.

So let me just tell you plainly. You did not complete me, because I was never a broken half waiting for a matching piece — but you made the life I already had worth documenting. Before you, my days blurred into each other. Now I find myself remembering Tuesdays. I remember the color of the sky on a random Wednesday because you pointed at it and said it looked like the inside of a seashell. You gave my ordinary weeks a reason to be remembered, and that is a strange, quiet kind of magic that nobody warns you about when they talk about love.

I love the parts of you that are easy to love — your laugh that starts before the joke is even finished, the way you narrate your own thoughts out loud without meaning to, how fiercely you defend the people you care about. But if I am honest, I have grown to love the harder parts even more: your bad days, your doubts, the mornings you wake up convinced you are somehow too much or not enough. I love those because you let me see them. You didn't perform a version of yourself for me. You handed me the real one, unfinished edges and all, and trusted me to stay. I don't think you understand what a gift that is. I am not going anywhere near it carelessly.

I am sorry for every time I made you feel like you were asking for too much when all you were asking for was my attention. I am sorry for the evenings I let my tiredness sound like disinterest. You deserve a love that doesn't need to be double-checked, and I promise you I am building exactly that, one ordinary Tuesday at a time — not through grand declarations, but through showing up, again and again, especially on the days it would be easier not to.

Here is my promise, the only one that matters: I will keep choosing you on the boring days, not just the beautiful ones. I will learn your silences well enough to know which ones need space and which ones need my hand. I will never let comfort turn into carelessness. And when the world is unkind to you, which it sometimes will be, I want to be the softest place you know how to land.

I think about our future the way other people think about their favorite memory — already fond of it, even though it hasn't happened yet. I picture small apartments and bigger ones, arguments about nothing that end in laughing, a home that smells like whatever you're cooking badly on purpose because you know it makes me laugh. I picture growing older next to you and somehow still being nervous before I kiss you. That is the whole dream. It was never complicated. It was always just you, for a very long time.

So happy Girlfriend Day, Anjani. Thank you for choosing me on your good days and, harder still, on your bad ones. Thank you for being the reason I now believe steady, ordinary love is the most romantic thing there is.

Forever Yours,
Jigar ❤
