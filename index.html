Girlfriend Day, this year — and every year after it

My Anjani,

I've rewritten this letter more times than anything else I've ever written, because every draft feels too small for what I actually feel. So let me just say it plainly.

Before you, my days blurred into each other. Now I remember Tuesdays. I remember the color of an ordinary sky because you pointed at it. You gave my life a reason to be paid attention to — and I love the small, unfiltered parts of you most: your laugh that starts before the joke ends, the way you narrate your own thoughts out loud, how fiercely you show up for people.

I'm sorry for the nights my tiredness sounded like distance. You deserve a love that doesn't need to be double-checked, and I'm building exactly that — one ordinary day at a time.

Here's my promise: I'll keep choosing you on the boring days, not just the beautiful ones. I'll learn your silences well enough to know which need space and which need my hand. When the world is unkind to you, I want to be the softest place you know how to land.

I think about our future the way people think of their favorite memory — already fond of it, even though it hasn't happened yet. Small apartments, silly arguments that end in laughing, growing old still nervous before I kiss you. That's the whole dream. It was always just you.

So happy Girlfriend Day, Anjani. Thank you for choosing me on the good days, and harder still, on the bad ones.

Forever Yours,
Jigar ❤
